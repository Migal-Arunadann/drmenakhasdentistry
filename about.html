Dr. P. Menakhasundhari completed her Master of Dental Surgery (M.D.S.) at the prestigious Tamilnadu Government Dental College and Hospital, Chennai — the apex government dental institution in the state, recognized for rigorous clinical training, high patient diversity, and exacting standards of prosthodontic craftsmanship.

As a qualified Prosthodontist and Implantologist (Regd No : 23712), her clinical focus centers on the functional restoration and aesthetic replacement of missing or severely damaged teeth. Whether addressing single tooth loss with dental implants, rehabilitating worn dentition with precision crowns and bridges, or fitting complete dentures for seniors, her clinical approach emphasizes anatomical harmony, bite balance, and long-term tissue preservation.

What Patients Can Expect

Consultation without commercial pressure.

We operate under core principles of clinical honesty and patient-first care:

01

Detailed Clinical Examination

Every consultation begins with careful direct inspection of your teeth, gums, and occlusion. We listen to your symptoms, history, and goals before formulating any plan.

02

Transparent Discussion of Treatment Options

When teeth require replacement or restoration, Dr. Menakhasundhari outlines the clinical benefits and maintenance requirements of each viable approach — without pushing unnecessary interventions.

03

Disciplined Infection Control

Strict sterilization protocols are followed for all handpieces, instruments, and operatory surfaces between every patient appointment.

04

Evening Consultation Convenience

Operating Monday through Saturday from 5:00 PM to 9:30 PM allows patients to attend appointments comfortably after office, school, or business hours.
