Category 01

Prosthodontics & Tooth Replacement

Prosthodontics is the specialized branch of dentistry devoted to restoring and replacing damaged or lost teeth using crowns, fixed bridges, and dentures.

Dental Crowns (Caps)

Consult on WhatsApp →

Full-coverage ceramic and zirconia crowns designed to protect structurally compromised teeth, fractured cusps, or teeth that have undergone root canal treatment. Custom-milled for accurate marginal fit, bite alignment, and natural tooth appearance.

Zirconia Crowns Ceramic Restorations Post-Endodontic Crowns

Fixed Dental Bridges

Consult on WhatsApp →

A fixed solution for bridging the gap created by one or more missing teeth. Anchored securely onto neighboring abutment teeth, bridges restore masticatory function, prevent shifting of adjacent teeth, and maintain facial profile.

Multi-unit Bridges Bite Stabilization

Complete & Partial Dentures

Consult on WhatsApp →

Custom-molded full dentures for complete arch tooth loss, as well as cast-partial and flexible acrylic partial dentures. Fabricated with meticulous attention to border molding, soft tissue comfort, and balanced occlusion for senior patients.

Full Arch Dentures Cast Partial Dentures Geriatric Care
Category 02

Dental Implantology

இம்பிளான்ட் நிபுணர் (Implant Specialist)

Advanced tooth replacement that substitutes the missing tooth root with a biocompatible fixture, providing permanent anchor for lifelike crowns.

Single Tooth Dental Implants

Consult on WhatsApp →

Ideal when a single tooth has been extracted due to trauma, decay, or root fracture. Unlike traditional bridges, a dental implant replaces the tooth without requiring preparation or trimming of healthy adjacent enamel.

Titanium Implants Bone Conservation Custom Abutments

Implant-Supported Bridges & Overdentures

Consult on WhatsApp →

For multiple missing teeth or loose complete dentures, implant-supported prostheses offer enhanced stability, eliminating denture slippage while stimulating healthy alveolar bone.

Multi-Tooth Replacement Implant Overdentures
